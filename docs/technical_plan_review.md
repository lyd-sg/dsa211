## Numerical feasibility and implementation checks

The proposed unscaled, unpenalised Logit is technically supported by the installed scikit-learn 1.9.1 API: `C=np.inf` specifies no penalty. Use that documented equivalent without a deprecated `penalty` argument. Newton-CG is a reasonable initial numerical method for disparate scales; it does not guarantee that a finite maximum-likelihood estimate exists.

Rare dummy levels can cause separation; finite coefficients and an absence of warnings alone do not prove a finite, well-identified MLE. At the **mandatory training-model review gate**, inspect actual convergence/iteration records, coefficient and probability finiteness, rank/conditioning evidence, and categorical-level outcome support in every training fold, especially pure rare levels. A pure level is a separation warning; absence of pure levels does not rule out multivariable separation. Where a boundary-MLE or other convergence concern occurs, explicitly report it and assess objective/prediction stability under tighter tolerances or greater iterations for the same statistical specification before accepting scores. Distinguish a stable finite-iterate numerical prediction from a certified finite MLE; do not make the latter claim without supporting evidence. Numerical retries may change iteration limits or unpenalised solver, but cannot quietly introduce scaling, penalties or predictor deletion. Preserve a real failed row and escalate a genuine baseline-feasibility conflict rather than fabricating successful estimation. These are required acceptance checks under the PRD's numerical-failure transparency, not permission to redefine the unpenalised baseline. This review has not established convergence on the data.

Standard SVD LDA is feasible for a dense modest-width design and can address rank deficiency without authorising a different baseline; actual warnings and degeneracy still require inspection. GaussianNB and BernoulliNB are technically implementable with the prescribed encodings, whose likelihood shortcomings are methodological limitations rather than permission to exclude them from fair comparison. The mixed-NB extension requires particular inspection of unknown-category denominators, likelihood components and the single class prior.

The final audit must independently reconcile saved predictions, confusion totals, fold/sample counts, metric calculations and revenue arithmetic; inspect each actual figure and interpretation; and verify that the test-producing cell did not run until the manifest was frozen. Post-audit edits may correct presentation using saved evidence. Any modelling defect discovered after test access must be disclosed rather than silently producing a replacement supposedly untouched audit.
